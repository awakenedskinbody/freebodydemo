# freebodydemo.com

One-page site where people book a free demo: weight loss (herbalist + Invisa-RED + before and after measurements) or face lift (consultation). The domain goes on the street signs.

- Hosting: Netlify project `demo-awakened-skin-body`, auto-deploys from `main`.
- Booking: Cal.com, embedded inline. The event links are at the top of the script in `index.html` (`CAL`): weight loss in English and Spanish, and face lift. Each calendar loads only when its section gets close to the screen.
- Cal.com events: `free-demo-weight-loss` (English), `free-demo-weight-loss-es` (Spanish) and `free-demo-face-lift` are copies of the Invisa-RED and Consultation events, kept separate so bookings from the signs can be counted and the awakened.skin events stay untouched. All three use the Cal.com availability schedule "Demo days" (weekly hours off, date overrides for Oct 22 to 25).
- Booking form: Name, Email and Phone are all required on the three demo events (Cal.com, Booking form tab).
- Reminders: three Cal.com email workflows, "Demo reminder 72 hours before", "24 hours before" and "3 hours before", each switched on for all three demo events (Workflows tab of each event). Cal's own confirmation email covers the moment of booking. The wording is Cal's standard "Reminder" template, which is locked on our current Cal plan (a custom template needs a paid plan). Sender name is set to "Awakened Skin + Body". Because the body can't be edited, the studio phone number rides in the event Location text ("2106 NE 123rd Street, Miami, FL 33181. Call or text (305) 909-7069", Spanish: "Llama o escribe al"), which Cal prints in every reminder. Do not attach these workflows to the awakened.skin events. The older "Untitled" workflow is unused and left off.
- Demo days: the dated line at the top hides itself after `DEMO_DAYS_END`. To open more days, add date overrides to the "Demo days" schedule in Cal.com and move `DEMO_DAYS_END`.
- Languages: English and Spanish on the same page. The page opens in Spanish when the phone is set to Spanish, or with `?lang=es` in the link.
- Tracking: GA4 `G-N31V27CTCY`, Google Ads `AW-18174427039`, Meta Pixel `2112349302961771`. Events: `cta_click`, `language_switch`, `demo_booked` with `demo` = weight_loss or face_lift (GA4); `Contact` and `Schedule` (Meta).
- Before and after carousels: one photo at a time, inside each demo card at the top of the page (`data-results` blocks in `index.html`). Photos are in `assets/results/` (`wl-*` for weight loss, `face-*` for face lift), resized from the photos on awakened.skin /results (our own clients, shared with consent). Photo rules: Invisa-RED only uses photos whose headline says "Body Contouring" (no cellulite, stretch marks or skin tightening headlines). The face lift set uses Fusion Plasma and Fibroblast photos, none that say "No Downtime" (we never claim zero downtime for warm plasma). The set is the 6 photos Sarah picked on Oct 15; two of her 8 were held back for those rules (the "Mini Lift, No Downtime" poster and the Invisa-RED "Stretch Marks" poster). To swap a photo, replace the file and keep the 800 x 1067 size. If you add or remove a photo, add or remove its `<li class="hc-slide">` and update the "1 / N" counter text next to it.
- Face lift demo: includes a mini face lift with atmospheric plasma ("no needles, no fillers, no toxins"). It never says zero downtime.
- Copy rules: "Weight loss" names the demo program (herbalist plan); Invisa-RED's own sentences talk about circumference reduction of the waist, hips and thighs only (never "inches", fat, cellulite, stretch marks or skin tightening), and "FDA-cleared laser" stays in its own sentence, never next to weight loss. The Florida s. 456.062 statement stays in the footer because the page advertises a free service.
