# freebodydemo.com

One-page site where people book a free demo: weight loss (herbalist + Invisa-RED + before and after measurements). The domain goes on the street signs.

**Body demo only since Oct 20, 2026.** The face lift calendar sold out, so the face lift card, section, FAQ line and Cal embed were removed from the page. The two-demo version (weight loss and face lift) is saved untouched on the branch `backup/two-demos-2026-10-20` (commit 31f10d2). To bring it back: `git checkout backup/two-demos-2026-10-20 -- index.html` (the face photos `assets/results/face-*.jpg` are still in the repo), then commit and push. Everything below that talks about face lift describes that backup version, not what is live.

- Hosting: Netlify project `demo-awakened-skin-body`, auto-deploys from `main`.
- Booking: Cal.com, embedded inline. The event links are at the top of the script in `index.html` (`CAL`): weight loss in English and Spanish (the face lift link is only in the backup branch). Each calendar loads only when its section gets close to the screen.
- Cal.com events: `free-demo-weight-loss` (English), `free-demo-weight-loss-es` (Spanish) and `free-demo-face-lift` are copies of the Invisa-RED and Consultation events, kept separate so bookings from the signs can be counted and the awakened.skin events stay untouched. All three use the Cal.com availability schedule "Demo days" (weekly hours off, date overrides for Oct 22 to 25).
- Booking form: Name, Email and Phone are all required on the three demo events (Cal.com, Booking form tab).
- Reminders: three Cal.com email workflows, "Demo reminder 72 hours before", "24 hours before" and "3 hours before", each switched on for all three demo events (Workflows tab of each event). Cal's own confirmation email covers the moment of booking. The wording is Cal's standard "Reminder" template, which is locked on our current Cal plan (a custom template needs a paid plan). Sender name is set to "Awakened Skin + Body". Because the body can't be edited, the studio phone number rides in the event Location text ("2106 NE 123rd Street, Miami, FL 33181. Call or text (305) 909-7069", Spanish: "Llama o escribe al"), which Cal prints in every reminder. Do not attach these workflows to the awakened.skin events. The older "Untitled" workflow is unused and left off.
- Demo days banner (Oct 21): the amber box at the top of the hero (`#demo-dates`, class `dates soldout`) says Oct 22, 23 and 24 are sold out and Sunday Oct 25 is open, 9 AM to 7 PM, in English and Spanish. Edit the sentence there when availability changes. It hides itself after `DEMO_DAYS_END`. The meta description and og text carry the same message.
- Demo days: the dated line at the top hides itself after `DEMO_DAYS_END`. To open more days, add date overrides to the "Demo days" schedule in Cal.com and move `DEMO_DAYS_END`.
- Languages: English and Spanish on the same page. The page opens in Spanish when the phone is set to Spanish, or with `?lang=es` in the link.
- Tracking: GA4 `G-N31V27CTCY`, Google Ads `AW-18174427039`, Meta Pixel `2112349302961771`. Events: `cta_click`, `language_switch`, `demo_booked` with `demo` = weight_loss (GA4; the backup branch also sends face_lift); `Contact` and `Schedule` (Meta).
- Before and after carousels: one photo at a time, inside each demo card at the top of the page (`data-results` blocks in `index.html`). Photos are in `assets/results/` (`wl-*` for weight loss, `face-*` for face lift), resized from the photos on awakened.skin /results and Sarah's uploads (our own clients, shared with consent). The cream frame that comes with each poster is cropped off (frame only, never the photo) so they sit cleanly on the dark cards. Photo rules: Sarah decided on Oct 15 to drop the old restrictions, so the "Mini Lift, No Downtime" poster and the Invisa-RED "Stretch Marks" poster are in. Still true: Invisa-RED copy talks about circumference reduction only, and no photo gets a caption that claims fat loss, cellulite or skin tightening. Rachel gives the final nod on which photos stay. The 12 photos: `wl-01..03` and `face-01..03` are the first six Sarah picked, `wl-04` is Stretch Marks and `face-04` is Plasma Current Mini Lift, No Downtime (both from her second upload), and `wl-05` (female side view), `wl-06` (side view in jeans), `face-05` (Mini Lift eye close-up, two weeks later) and `face-06` (Face and Neck, one month later) are the 4 Sarah circled from the awakened.skin /results set. To swap a photo, replace the file, keep the 800 x 1067 size, and bump the `?v=` number on its `src` in `index.html` (Netlify tells browsers to keep /assets files for 7 days, so a same-name file would show the old photo until then). If you add or remove a photo, add or remove its `<li class="hc-slide">` and update the "01 / 06" style counter text next to it (the script formats the count after that).
- Face lift demo (backup branch only): includes a mini face lift with atmospheric plasma ("no needles, no fillers, no toxins"). The poster in the carousel says "No Downtime" (Sarah approved it); page copy itself still says minimal to no downtime for most, up to 48 hours for a few.
- Look (Oct 17): follows the awakened.skin v6 system (see `claude/Website_Rebuild_v6_Notes.md` in the project): dark ink hero with a drifting plasma glow (two small canvases, off for reduced motion), tight DM Sans headlines with one Cormorant italic accent word, DM Mono uppercase labels, rectangular buttons with an amber primary, floating pill nav that turns dark glass over dark sections, blur-in reveals. Palette is the brand set plus amber `#E08A2E`. All of it is in the `<style>` block and the last `<script>` at the bottom of `index.html` (look only, separate from the booking and tracking script). On desktop the headline sits on the left and the one demo card on the right; between 760px and 1179px the card goes wide (photo left, words right) under the headline; below 760px everything stacks. Every Book button (nav, card, sticky bar on phones) jumps straight to the calendar (`#pick-time`).
- Headline (Oct 17): "Don't take our word for it. Try it free." (ES: "No tienes que creernos. Pruébalo gratis."). It replaced "Free demos at our studio, right off the Broad Causeway", which now lives in the line under it and in the address line.
- Copy rules: "Weight loss" names the demo program (herbalist plan); Invisa-RED's own sentences talk about circumference reduction of the waist, hips and thighs only (never "inches", fat, cellulite, stretch marks or skin tightening), and "FDA-cleared laser" stays in its own sentence, never next to weight loss. The Florida s. 456.062 statement stays in the footer because the page advertises a free service.
